title: "Errors, limits, and compliance"
---

The reference for what can go wrong and what is enforced: the one error
shape every `/api/imessage/*` route uses, the full code table, the limits
(new contacts, sizes, timeouts), and the consent rules you are responsible
for.

## Error shape

Every error on `/api/imessage/*` is JSON with a machine-readable `code`:

```json
{"error": {"code": "new_contact_limit",
           "message": "This line can message at most 3 new contacts per 15m (3 already in this window). Existing conversations are not limited. Try again in 412 s.",
           "limit": 3, "window": "15m", "used": 3, "retry_after_s": 412},
 "detail": "This line can message at most 3 new contacts per 15m …"}
```

Branch on `error.code`, show `error.message` to a human. Rate-limited
responses also carry a `Retry-After` header (`2` for `line_busy`, `30` for `rate_limited`). A
validation error carries `error.fields`, a list of `{"field", "message"}`.
A failed send or reaction keeps its `record` (the stored row with
`status: "failed"`) and `id` next to the error, plus the line's `status`
(`busy`, `offline`, `timeout`, or `error`).

| HTTP | `code` | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 401 | `unauthorized` | Bad or missing bearer. | Check the token. |
| 402 | `payment_required` | The line's subscription is not active. | Subscribe in the console. |
| 402 | `ai_credits_exhausted` | `POST /api/imessage/agent/start` when the account has fewer [credits](/imessage/ai-agent#credits) left than the first message needs, or a video send with fewer left than the video costs (1 per 3 MB). Nothing was sent. `error.left`, `error.needed` and `error.buy_url` say how many are left, how many it needs, and where to buy more (`error.resets_at` is `null`: nothing refills). | Buy more at `error.buy_url`, or send fewer first-message texts. |
| 403 | `forbidden` | Not a line account, or a line token on a non-iMessage route. | — |
| 403 | `recipient_opted_out` | They texted STOP (see below). `error.handle` and `error.opted_out_at` say who and when. | Don't retry. Only their START opts them back in. |
| 403 | `awaiting_reply` | You already sent this contact 2 messages and they have never replied. Messages sent within 5 minutes of each other count as one, so a greeting plus a link is one. Once they send anything back (a text, photo, video, voice note or tapback), the limit is gone for good. `error.handle` and `error.unanswered` say who and how many. | Wait for their first reply. |
| 403 | `attachment_before_reply` | A photo, video, voice message or poll to a contact who has never sent this number anything. Nothing was sent or stored. `error.handle` and `error.kind` (`image`, `video`, `audio` or `poll`) say who and what. | Send text until they reply. |
| 403 | `managed_by_zerosettle` | A send from Close or HubSpot would come from a line managed by the AI agent: it answers there. With several numbers, a new conversation has to name a line managed by API. `POST /api/imessage/send` and MCP `send_imessage` aren't refused. | Send it with `POST /api/imessage/send`, start it with `POST /api/imessage/agent/start`, or set the group back to `managed_by: "api"`. |
| 404 | `not_found` | No such message on this line, no stored recording / photo / video / poster / profile photo for that route, or a video id (`GET /api/imessage/videos/{id}`, or `video_id` on `/send` or `/broadcast`) that isn't one of your uploads. Nothing was sent. | — |
| 409 | `conflict` | E.g. reacting to a message that was never delivered (no GUID). | — |
| 409 | `no_conversation` | Typing at a handle the line has never exchanged a message with. | Send a message first. |
| 409 | `poll_unsupported` | A poll from a line that requires an update to send polls. Nothing was sent or stored. | Send text, or try again after the update. |
| 409 | `video_unsupported` | A video when videos are disabled for the account, or the line requires an update to send videos. Nothing was sent, stored or charged. | Send a photo or a link, or contact ZeroSettle about availability or the update. |
| 409 | `video_processing` | A `video_id` still processing after the send waited a minute for it (a broadcast waits only a few seconds). `error.video_id` names the video (for a `video_b64`, the upload it became). Nothing was sent. | Try again in a few seconds. |
| 413 | `video_too_large` | A video upload over 100 MB. | Trim or compress it first. |
| 422 | `video_invalid` | A video that isn't MP4/MOV/WebM or can't be read, has no picture or runs longer than a minute, or a `video_id` that failed processing. `error.message` says which. Nothing was sent. | Fix the file and upload it again. |
| 409 | `no_profile` | Sharing the name and photo before setting them. | `PUT /api/imessage/profile` first. |
| 409 | `not_managed` | `POST /api/imessage/agent/start` on a line group managed by API. | `PUT /api/imessage/agent` with `managed_by: "zerosettle"` first. |
| 502 | `agent_unavailable` | `POST /api/imessage/agent/start` with `first_message_enabled: false`: the agent couldn't write the opening text just now. Nothing was sent. | Try again, or turn `first_message` back on. |
| 404 | `unknown_line` | The `line_id` or `line` given is not one of your account's lines. | Use an `id` (or `number`) from `lines` in `GET /api/imessage/line`. |
| 404 | `unknown_group` | The `group` (or `group_id`) given is not one of your account's line groups. | Use a `group_id` from `lines` in `GET /api/imessage/line`. |
| 403 | `group_chats_not_enabled` | A [group chat](/imessage/group-chats) call on a number without group chats. Nothing was sent. | Group chats are on for every number; contact ZeroSettle support, which may have turned them off for this one. |
| 404 | `unknown_chat` | No line on your account has that `chat_id`. Nothing was sent. | Use a `chat_id` from `GET /api/imessage/groups`. |
| 409 | `client_update_required` | A group chat call to a line that requires an update to support group chats. Nothing was sent. | Try again after the update. |
| 400 | `invalid_code` | [Adding a line](/imessage/your-imessage-number#add-a-line) with a discount code that isn't valid. | Check the code, or leave it out. |
| 402 | `payment_failed` | Adding a line: the card was declined. Nothing was charged. | Update the card in the console, then retry. |
| 403 | `owner_only` | Adding a line with a team member's key. | Use the account owner's key. |
| 409 | `no_capacity` | Adding a line: not enough numbers are free right now. Nothing was charged. | Try fewer, or try again later. |
| 409 | `add_number_unavailable` | Adding a line to an account without an active card subscription. | Contact ZeroSettle. |
| 409 | `line_draining` | The line named is being retired: it answers its conversations but opens no new one. `error.line_id` and `error.line` name it. | Send from another line, or leave the line out. |
| 409 | `ambiguous_line` | Deleting a conversation with a contact who is on more than one of your lines, without `?line_id=`. `error.line_ids` and `error.lines` list them. | Add `?line_id=`. |
| 422 | `invalid_request` | Bad `to`, `text`, `url`, `reaction`… `error.fields` lists them. | Fix the input. |
| 422 | `not_on_imessage` | Creating a group chat with a member who isn't on iMessage. `error.handle` says who. | Leave them out. |
| 429 | `line_busy` | The line was busy for the whole retry window. | Retry after 2 s. |
| 429 | `rate_limited` | A video upload (`POST /api/imessage/videos`, or a send with `video_b64`) while the account already has 3 videos processing, or 10 are processing across ZeroSettle. `Retry-After: 30`, `error.retry_after_s` 30. Nothing was stored or sent. | Wait for one to be `ready` (or 30 s), then retry. |
| 429 | `daily_limit` | The number has sent and received 10,000 messages today (see below). `error.used`, `error.limit`. | Wait `retry_after_s` (the next 3 AM Eastern). |
| 429 | `new_contact_limit` | The send would exceed the line's new-contact allowance (see below). | On Growth, wait `error.retry_after_s` before retrying. On Pro, ask the contact to text you first or [upgrade to Growth](https://portal.zerosettle.io/imessage#upgrade). |
| 429 | `agent_call_limit` | `POST /api/imessage/agent/start` with `first_message_enabled: false` when the AI agent was already asked to write to that contact 60 times today (UTC), counting the times it decided to send nothing. Nothing was sent. | Wait `retry_after_s`. |
| 503 | `phone_offline` / `phone_timeout` | The line is temporarily unreachable or did not answer. | Retry with backoff. |
| 503 | `unavailable` | Video storage is briefly unavailable (an upload, a video send, or fetching a video or its poster). Nothing was sent or charged. | Retry in a minute. |
| 502 | `send_failed` | The line could not send it (e.g. a bad handle). | Fix the input. |
| 502 | `typing_failed` | The line could not raise the typing indicator for that handle. | Fix the input; it is best-effort anyway. |

## Limits

Each number allows **10,000 messages a day, sent and received, with known
contacts** (people who texted it first, or whom it already has a conversation
with). Once a number has sent and received 10,000 messages since 3 AM
Eastern, every send from it is refused with `429 daily_limit` until the day
resets; incoming messages still arrive. The console and `GET /api/imessage/line` → `usage`
show the last 30 days' counts (rolling; texts, voice messages, photos, and
videos, not reactions) with `limit: null`. The other enforced allowances
are new contacts and unanswered messages, and, for the hosted AI agent and
for videos, credits:

| Usage | Allowance | Enforced? |
|---|---|---|
| Messages, sent and received | **10,000 / day** per number, reset at 3 AM Eastern | Yes — `429 daily_limit` |
| New contacts | **Pro** is inbound-only. **Growth** starts with **50 new contacts/day** across the account, with options up to **500/day**. On Growth, a number can start conversations with up to **3 new contacts per 15 minutes**. Daily limits reset at 3 AM Eastern. | Yes — `429 new_contact_limit` |
| Unanswered messages | **2** per contact until their first reply | Yes — `403 awaiting_reply` |
| Photos, videos, voice messages and polls | Only to contacts who have replied at least once | Yes — `403 attachment_before_reply` |
| Texts the hosted AI agent sends | **500 free credits** on Pro, **1,000** on Growth, once per account; packs add more ([credits](/imessage/ai-agent#credits)) | Yes — the agent goes quiet; `402 ai_credits_exhausted` on `/agent/start` |
| Videos | **1 credit per 3 MB** of each video sent (per recipient) or received, rounded up (a full minute is about 6), from the same credits; a received one is free when fewer are left | Yes — `402 ai_credits_exhausted` |

- Text: 1–4000 characters. Attachments: voice messages (6 MB), photos
  (5 MB, with a caption) and videos (up to a minute; 100 MB uploaded or 25
  MB as `video_b64`, sent as a 720p MP4 of at most 15 MB, with a caption) —
  see [Attachments](/imessage/attachments); tapbacks on any of them. Profile photo:
  JPEG or PNG, 3 MB. A received video is delivered up to 60 MB.
- Handles: E.164 phone numbers or iMessage email addresses, up to 128
  characters.
- **New contacts.** Pro lets you reply to people who text you first. To start
  conversations with opted-in contacts, [upgrade to Growth](https://portal.zerosettle.io/imessage#upgrade)
  for \$199/month per number. Check `usage.new_contacts.limit` in
  `GET /api/imessage/line` for your account's daily allowance. Growth also has
  a rolling limit of **3 new contacts per 15 minutes per number**. The daily
  count resets at 3 AM Eastern. A daily-limit response has `window: "day"`. A
  "new contact" is a handle the line delivers a text, voice message, photo,
  or video to **first**; reactions, typing, profile shares, and failed sends never
  count. Anyone who texted the line first never counts, and neither does an
  ongoing conversation; both share the number's 10,000 messages a day. This
  is what keeps the number out of Apple's spam filters; it applies to every caller, the console included. Deleting a
  conversation makes that handle a new contact again. With [more than one
  number](/imessage/your-imessage-number#more-than-one-number) each number has its
  own daily limit and `usage` shows the account's total.
  In a [group chat](/imessage/group-chats), each member new to the line counts as a
  new contact when you start the group or add them.
- **Unanswered messages**: at most **2 messages** to a contact who has never
  sent the line anything (a text, photo, video, voice note, or tapback). After their
  first reply there is no limit for that contact. Messages
  sent within 5 minutes of the first count as one, so a greeting plus a link
  is one; put the link in the same message when you can. A third is refused
  with `403 awaiting_reply` (nothing sent or stored; `error.handle` and
  `error.unanswered` say who and how many) until they first reply. Failed sends
  and the line's own tapbacks don't count. Repeated messages to people who
  never answer are the fastest way to get a number flagged by Apple: when
  that happens every send fails, to everyone.
- **Attachments wait for a reply**: until a contact has sent the line
  anything (a text, photo, video, voice note, or tapback), it can only text them:
  a photo, video or voice message is refused with `403 attachment_before_reply`
  (nothing sent or stored). So a new contact's first messages are text.
- One send at a time per line; concurrent sends queue behind each other and
  surface as `line_busy` after ~12 s of waiting. Typing calls share that
  lock but hold it for under 2 s.
- Typing: `ttl_s` at most 60 per call; not stored, not counted, not billed.
  Inbound typing events are delivered once with no retries.
- Inbound latency: about 1–2 s from the line receiving a text to your webhook.
  Inbound is pushed; nothing polls for it, so inbound never delays a send.
- Webhook: HTTPS only (HTTP for localhost), 10 s timeout, 3 retries, no redirects
  followed.

## Message retention and HIPAA

Automatic message expiration is currently unavailable. You can remove stored
messages using the [delete-conversation endpoint](/imessage/read-messages#delete-a-conversation).
Configurable message retention is planned as part of the HIPAA update at no
extra charge. The release date is unannounced.

HIPAA plan eligibility, pricing and availability are unannounced. You must
have a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with ZeroSettle before using
the service for protected health information (PHI). See the
[iMessage API Terms of Service](https://zerosettle.io/legal/imessage-terms/).

## Compliance — you need consent

<Warning>

Use ZeroSettle only to contact people who agreed to hear from
you for the intended purpose. Requirements depend on the message's purpose,
sending technology and jurisdiction. Establish consent independently of
the delivery method. Include opt-out instructions in your message when your workflow requires them.

</Warning>

- **United States:** the [FCC's delivery restrictions](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-L/section-64.1200)
  generally require prior express written consent for advertising or
  telemarketing covered by the autodialer rules. What qualifies as an
  autodialer depends on the equipment's capabilities; see the Supreme Court's
  [Facebook v. Duguid decision](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/20pdf/19-511_p86b.pdf).
  Do-not-call and state-law requirements may apply independently.
- **Canada (CASL), UK/EU (PECR, GDPR ePrivacy), Australia (Spam Act):** consent
  first, an easy opt-out in every campaign, and records of who agreed and when.
- **Carrier and Apple rules:** unsolicited volume gets a number filtered or
  deactivated. The new-contact limits above exist for this reason; they are a
  floor, not a licence.

What "consent" means in practice:

1. The person gave you their number **for this purpose** — a form, a checkbox,
   a purchase flow, a reply to you — and you can show the record.
2. Your first message says who you are.
3. **Honor "STOP"** immediately: never message that handle again unless they
   opt back in. ZeroSettle enforces this for you (below); keep your own
   opt-out list as well, for your other channels and your records.
4. Don't message before 8 am or after 9 pm in the recipient's time zone for
   anything promotional.

ZeroSettle suspends lines used for unsolicited messaging. If you are unsure
whether you have consent, you don't.

## STOP is enforced

When a contact texts **STOP** directly to your number, the server records it
and blocks every number on your account from reaching them:

- **What counts:** the whole message, or its first sentence, is one of
  `stop`, `stop all`, `stopall`, `unsubscribe`, `unsubscribe me`, `quit`,
  `opt out`, `optout`, `opt-out`, `stop please`, `please stop`,
  `stop texting me`, `stop messaging me`, or a supported phrase such as
  "take me off your list", "remove me", "don't text me", "no more texts",
  "wrong number" or "leave me alone". Matching uses the whole message or
  first sentence, ignoring case, spacing, apostrophes and end punctuation;
  a phrase appearing elsewhere in a longer sentence is not enough.
  `CANCEL` and `END` are not currently recognized opt-out keywords. This same
  parser applies to incoming SMS as well.
- In group chats, STOP and other opt-out keywords are delivered as ordinary
  messages, including when someone replies to or mentions your number.
- **What is blocked:** texts, voice messages, photos and videos (`POST /send`),
  reactions, typing indicators and name-and-photo shares to that handle all
  return `403 recipient_opted_out` with `error.handle` and
  `error.opted_out_at`. Nothing is sent and nothing is stored. The
  console disables the conversation and says when they texted STOP.
  A [group chat](/imessage/group-chats) with them in it is blocked too: sends into
  it are refused the same way until they are removed from it or text START.
- **You still receive everything.** The STOP itself, and anything they send
  afterwards, arrives on your webhook and stream like any other message.
- **Opting back in:** the contact texts **START** (or `unstop`). If they ask
  you some other way, contact ZeroSettle and we can clear it.
- **Who has opted out:** `GET /api/imessage/opt-outs` returns
  `{"opt_outs": [{"handle": "+18055559876", "opted_out_at": "2026-09-28T17:04:11Z"}]}`,
  and the [live stream](/imessage/live-stream-sse) sends an `optout` event on each STOP
  and START. If your account has more than one number, each opt-out and each
  `optout` event also carries `line` and `line_id`: the line the STOP or START
  came in on. To get them on your webhook too, register it with
  `"optout_events": true` ([opt-out events](/imessage/webhooks-step-by-step#opt-out-events)).
- **Only STOPs received since this enforcement launched are recorded.**
  Anyone who texted STOP before then is not on the list and is not blocked,
  so keep honoring those earlier opt-outs yourself.
