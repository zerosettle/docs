---
title: "Authentication"
---

Every API call takes a bearer token:

```
Authorization: Bearer pt_mcp_…
```

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `401` | Missing, invalid, or revoked token. |
| `402` | The line's subscription is not active. Subscribe (or resubscribe) in the console. (From `POST /api/imessage/agent/start` or a video send it can also be `ai_credits_exhausted`: the account is out of [credits](/imessage/ai-agent#credits).) |
| `403` | Valid token, but the account has no iMessage number (or you called a non-iMessage route with an iMessage token). |
| `422` | Body failed validation; `error.fields` lists each offending field with a message. |

Tokens are minted in the console (`/imessage`), which calls
`POST /account/mcp/token` with your Firebase sign-in. `DELETE /account/mcp/token`
revokes every token on the account.

Check that a token works and see your number's state:

```http
GET /api/imessage/line
```

<CodeGroup>

```bash cURL
curl -s -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" $BASE/api/imessage/line
```

</CodeGroup>

`handle` is the line's own number (the one people text); `phone` is the
number you were invited on. For Growth, `usage.new_contacts.limit` shows your
account's daily new-contact allowance. The example shows an allowance of 50.
`usage.new_contacts.resets_at` is when today's
new-contact count goes back to 0: the next 3 AM Eastern, in UTC. `profile` is `null` until a name and photo are
set; `last_delivery` is `null` until the first webhook attempt. An account
with videos enabled, a line group managed by the hosted AI agent, or past
credit spending or purchases also gets `ai_credits`, its
[credits](/imessage/ai-agent#credits). Videos are enabled by default for current and
future accounts; `"videos": true` appears when enabled. ZeroSettle can set an
individual account override. A number with
[group chats](/imessage/group-chats) on (every number, unless ZeroSettle turned them
off for it) also shows `"group_chats": true`; no other number has the key.
