title: "AI agent in the console"
---

Turn on AI replies without writing any code: the hosted AI agent answers
every text to your number from a business context you write. No server,
webhook or model of your own. This page walks through the console at
[portal.zerosettle.io/imessage](https://portal.zerosettle.io/imessage); for
the API, MCP and every field, see
[Managed by AI Agent](/imessage/ai-agent#managed-by-ai-agent-no-server).

## What changes

Each sender (its name and photo, and every number that shares it) is
**Managed by API** or **Managed by AI Agent**:

| | Managed by API | Managed by AI Agent |
|---|---|---|
| Incoming texts | sent to your webhook | answered by the AI agent |
| Typing a reply in the console | works | off |
| Sending from the API | works | works; the agent treats your texts as its own |
| Starting a conversation | send a message | the agent sends your first message |
| Credits | none for texts | one for each text the agent sends |

## Before you start

You need a paid number. Everything below happens in the console's
**Messaging** tab.

1. Sign in at [portal.zerosettle.io/imessage](https://portal.zerosettle.io/imessage).
2. Open the settings panel: the right-hand column on a computer, or the
   menu button on a phone.
3. Choose the **Messaging** tab.
4. With more than one sender, pick the one the agent should answer for
   under **Pick a sender**.

<Tip>

Rather not fill in the form? Once you click **Managed by AI
Agent**, the **Vibe code your iMessage agent** box has a prompt to paste
into Claude or ChatGPT. It asks about your business and sets up
everything below for you over MCP.

</Tip>

## Step 1: Switch to the AI agent

AI replies start the moment you confirm the switch. Until then the agent's
settings are shown greyed out and can't be edited.

1. Under **Who answers**, click **Managed by AI Agent**. The settings appear
   below, greyed out; your server still answers.
2. Click **Switch to AI agent**.
3. Read the confirmation (it says how many free credits you get) and click
   **Switch**.

From then on your webhook isn't called and the console's composer is off
until you switch back.

<Warning>

The business context starts as an example for a made-up
software business, and the agent would tell people about it. If the
confirmation says **Switch with the example business context?**, do
step 2 right away, or set everything up first with the Vibe code prompt.

</Warning>

## Step 2: Tell the agent about your business

The agent knows only what you write in **Business Context**, and never
makes up facts it doesn't give. Replace the example with your business and
click **Save**. Cover:

- who it is texting and why (new leads, customers, people who booked)
- what you sell, with prices and any offers it may make
- policies: refunds, cancellations, hours, shipping
- when to send people to a person, and how to reach one
- answers to common questions and objections
- what it must never say or promise
- a few short sample exchanges in your voice

Up to 40,000 characters. A short example:

```text
You are Bright Smile Dental's assistant, texting patients who asked
about an appointment on our website. Answer their questions and get
them booked.

About us: a family dental office at 120 Main St, Austin.
Open Mon-Fri, 8am-5pm.
Prices: cleaning and exam $129. New-patient special $99 (first visit
only). We take Delta Dental and Cigna.
Policies: cancel at least 24 hours ahead or there is a $50 fee.
Anyone with pain, swelling or an emergency: tell them to call
(512) 555-0142 right away.
Never give medical advice or quote prices not listed here.

Example:
Them: do you take kids?
You: We do! Kids are welcome from age 3. Want me to find you a time
this week?
```

Then pick how it texts under **Personality**. Each choice saves when you
click it.

| Setting | Options | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Personality | Bubbly salesperson, Zoomer, Super casual, Friend, Professional, or Make your own | Friend |
| Response time | Instant, Human-like (5 to 10 min), Busy (30 to 60 min), Don't Reply (first message only) | Instant |

## Step 3: Test it from your phone

1. Set **Response time** to **Instant** while you test.
2. In Messages, text your number with a question a real customer would ask, such as a price or your hours.
3. You'll see "…" typing, then one to three texts. The conversation shows
   up in the console's inbox.
4. Ask something your business context doesn't cover. It should say it
   doesn't know or send you to a person, not guess.
5. Fix anything that sounds wrong in **Business Context**, click **Save**,
   and text again.

When you're happy, set **Response time** to the speed you want.

## Optional settings

The agent works with a business context alone. These boxes, further down
the Messaging tab, let it do more.

| Box | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Pick Purchases, Trial starts or Demos booked and add a link, such as a Stripe payment link or a booking page. The agent shares it when they're interested. | on, Purchases, no link |
| First message | 1 to 3 texts sent exactly as written when the agent starts a conversation. End on a question. Off: the agent writes its own opener. | on, a two-text opener |
| Connectors: Files | Price lists, menus, catalogs, FAQs or policies (PDF, Excel, CSV, Word or text, up to 10 MB). The agent reads every file before it answers. | none |
| Connectors: Add MCP server | Lets the agent use your tools: look up an order, check availability, book a time. Paste the server's link, and an access token if it needs one. | none |
| Triggers | Texts the agent sends on its own. **Once**: some days after a conversation's last message (a follow-up). **Every day**: in every conversation. Each lands in the morning (9–11), afternoon (1–3) or evening (6–8). | one follow-up a day after they go quiet |

MCP tools that only look things up start on. Ones that can change
something start off: anyone who texts your number can ask the agent to use
them, so turn those on only when that's safe.

## Start a conversation

The agent answers whoever texts you. To have it text someone first, use the
**Start a conversation** box: enter their phone number, and their name if
you know it (the agent uses it), then click **Start**. It sends your first
message, or writes its own opener, and answers whatever they reply. Each
text uses a credit, and the new-contact limits apply.

Everyone you've started with is listed under it, newest first, with whether
they've replied. It shows five; **Show all** opens the rest, and a row opens
that conversation. From code it's `POST /api/imessage/agent/start` or MCP
`start_conversation` ([details](/imessage/ai-agent#managed-by-ai-agent-no-server)).

## Credits and limits

Each text the agent sends uses one credit. Free credits come once, when
the account first uses them, and never expire.

| | Pro | Growth |
|---|---|---|
| Free credits (once) | 500 | 1,000 |
| New contacts a day, per number | 5 | 50 |

- What's left shows in the top bar and in the **Credits** box on the
  Profile tab. A banner appears when you're low and when you're out.
- **Buy credits** adds 1,000 for $50 or 5,000 for $200.
- People who text you first don't count as new contacts.
- The agent honors STOP and sends at most 2 messages to someone who hasn't
  replied.

More in [Credits](/imessage/ai-agent#credits).

## Troubleshooting

To pause the agent, click **Managed by API** under **Who answers** and
confirm **Switch to API**.

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The settings are greyed out | you're previewing; your server still answers | click **Switch to AI agent** |
| It talks about "Example App" | the example business context is still saved | replace it in **Business Context** and click **Save** |
| It doesn't answer at all | out of credits, or the subscription isn't paid | **Buy credits**, or update your card under Settings → Billing |
| It answers late | **Response time** isn't Instant | pick **Instant** under Personality |
| It went quiet with one person | 60 replies to them today | it answers again after midnight UTC |
| It skipped a reply | they said something like "thanks!" after it wrapped up | nothing; that's on purpose |
| It got a fact wrong or dodged a question | the fact isn't in your context or files | add it to **Business Context** or upload a file |
| You can't type in the console | the sender is managed by the AI agent | switch to **Managed by API**, or send with the API |
| Saving the business context fails | it's over 40,000 characters | shorten it, or move long lists into a file |
| Starting a conversation hits a limit | today's new contacts are used up | wait until tomorrow, or upgrade to Growth for 50 a day |
