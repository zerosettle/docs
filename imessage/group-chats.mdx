---
title: "Group chats"
---

Every number can start an iMessage group with up to
31 people, send into it, read it, and rename it, add or remove members, or
leave it. Groups the number is added to by someone else work the same way.
Everything else in this reference still applies; this page covers what is
different in a group.

## Availability

Group chats are **on for every number**, current and new; there is nothing
to request or switch on. A number with them on shows `"group_chats": true`
in `GET /api/imessage/line` (and on its entry in `lines`). ZeroSettle can
turn them off for an individual number; such a number has no `group_chats`
key at all, and nothing it returns changes. On a number without them:

- every group call (creating a group, sending or typing with `chat_id`,
  updating or leaving a group, reacting or replying to a group message) is
  refused with `403 group_chats_not_enabled` ("Group chats are not enabled
  on this number."), and nothing is sent;
- messages people send in a group that includes the number are not reported
  as group messages: they carry no `chat_id`.

A line that requires an update to support group chats answers
`409 client_update_required` without sending. Try again after the update.

## The chat id

A group is named by its `chat_id`, a string such as
`iMessage;+;chat123456789` (at most 128 characters). Store it as it is; it
never changes for the life of the group, even when the group is renamed or
its members change. In a URL, encode it
(`iMessage%3B%2B%3Bchat123456789`).

Wherever a 1:1 conversation is keyed by `handle`, a group is keyed by its
`chat_id`: a conversation is either one contact (`handle`) or one group
(`chat_id`), on one line. A group lives on the line that is in it; calls that
name a `chat_id` act on that line, so `line` / `line_id` are not needed
(`404 unknown_chat` when none of your lines has that group).

## Start a group

```http
POST /api/imessage/groups
Content-Type: application/json
{"to": ["+18055559876", "+13105550000", "jane@example.com"], "text": "Welcome to the launch group!", "name": "Launch crew"}
```

- `to`: 2–31 handles (phone numbers or emails, each as in `/send`), the
  other members. Your number is always in the group; don't list it.
- `text` (required): the first message, 1–4000 characters after trimming.
  The group is created by sending it.
- `name` (optional): the group's name, up to 60 characters. Without one,
  Messages shows the members' names.
- `line_id` / `line` (optional, an account with
  [more than one number](/imessage/your-imessage-number#choosing-the-line)): the
  number that creates the group. It must have group chats on.

Every member is checked as if you were texting them 1:1, and nothing is
sent if any check fails:

- **STOP:** a member who texted STOP to any of your numbers →
  `403 recipient_opted_out` (`error.handle` says who).
- **New contacts.** Use Growth to start a group with people who haven't
  messaged your number. Each new member **counts as one new contact** under the
  line's daily allowance. The API returns `429 new_contact_limit` if the group
  would exceed that allowance.
- **On iMessage:** each new member must be on iMessage → `422
  not_on_imessage` (`error.handle` says who).

Response (`200`), the shape of a send plus the group:

```json
{
  "ok": true, "status": "message_sent", "message": "…", "id": 70,
  "record": {"id": 70, "kind": "message", "direction": "out", "handle": "iMessage;+;chat123456789",
             "text": "Welcome to the launch group!", "status": "sent", "guid": "7B1D…",
             "ts_ms": 1757400200000, "chat_id": "iMessage;+;chat123456789"},
  "chat": {"chat_id": "iMessage;+;chat123456789", "name": "Launch crew",
           "participants": ["+18055559876", "+13105550000", "jane@example.com"]}
}
```

`participants` are always the **other** members, never your own number. With
several numbers, `chat` also carries `line` and `line_id`.

**Sent, group id pending.** Rarely, the first message goes out to everyone
before the group's id is known. The answer is still `200` with `"ok": true`,
but `"status": "group_unconfirmed"` and `"chat": null`. The message is
stored and counted (`record`), and each member counts as a contact, so
**don't send it again**: a retry starts a second group. The group shows up,
with its real `chat_id`, in `GET /api/imessage/groups` (and a `group.updated`
on the live stream) once someone in it replies.

## Send to a group

`POST /api/imessage/send` with `chat_id` instead of `to` (pass exactly one
of them):

```bash
curl -s -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"chat_id":"iMessage;+;chat123456789","text":"Doors open at 7"}' $BASE/api/imessage/send
```

- Everything a 1:1 send takes works in a group: `text`, `image_b64` /
  `audio_b64`, `video_id` / `video_b64`, `poll`, `reply_to` and `effect`. A
  video into a group uses its [credits](/imessage/ai-agent#credits) (1 per 3 MB) once
  per send, however many members it has.
- The response is a normal send response; its `record` carries `chat_id`
  and its `handle` is the chat id.
- **STOP:** if any member has texted STOP, the send is refused with
  `403 recipient_opted_out` (`error.handle` says who) and nothing is sent.
  Remove them from the group, or leave it.
- The unanswered-message rule (`awaiting_reply`), the on-iMessage check and
  the automatic name-and-photo share are 1:1 rules: a group send skips them.

## Read groups and their messages

```http
GET /api/imessage/groups
```

```json
{"groups": [{"chat_id": "iMessage;+;chat123456789", "name": "Launch crew",
             "participants": ["+18055559876", "+13105550000", "jane@example.com"],
             "created_at_ms": 1757400200000, "updated_at_ms": 1757400200000}]}
```

Every group on your numbers that have group chats on (with `line` and
`line_id` when you have several numbers). `name` is `null` when the group has
none. Groups your number has left are not listed.

**Messages.** A group message has the same shape as any other, plus
`chat_id`; a 1:1 message never has the key:

```json
{"id": 71, "kind": "message", "direction": "in", "handle": "+13105550000", "text": "See you there",
 "status": "received", "guid": "8C2E…", "ts_ms": 1757400230000, "chat_id": "iMessage;+;chat123456789"}
```

- **Inbound:** `handle` is the member who sent it.
- **Outbound:** `handle` is the chat id.
- `GET /api/imessage/messages?chat_id=…` returns one group's messages
  (`chat_id` and `handle` can't be combined). `?handle=` returns only the
  1:1 conversation with that contact, never their group messages; without
  either, you get everything, groups included.

**Conversations.** `GET /api/imessage/conversations` lists each group as one
conversation, next to the 1:1 ones:

```json
{"handle": "iMessage;+;chat123456789", "chat_id": "iMessage;+;chat123456789", "name": "Launch crew",
 "participants": ["+18055559876", "+13105550000", "jane@example.com"], "count": 12,
 "last": {"id": 71, "direction": "in", "text": "See you there", "status": "received", "ts_ms": 1757400230000}}
```

Branch on `chat_id`: when it is present, the entry is a group and `handle`
is its chat id. A group your number has left (or was removed from) is still
listed with its messages, with `"left": true`; nothing can be sent into it
(`404 unknown_chat`). `DELETE /api/imessage/conversations/{handle}` with an
encoded chat id deletes that group's stored messages; like a 1:1 delete, nothing is
unsent and nobody in the group is told.

## Rename, add, remove, leave

```http
POST /api/imessage/groups/update
Content-Type: application/json
{"chat_id": "iMessage;+;chat123456789", "name": "Launch crew 2026", "add": ["+16465550199"], "remove": ["jane@example.com"]}
```

- `name` (optional): the new name, up to 60 characters.
- `add` / `remove` (optional): handles to add to, or remove from, the
  group. A member added who has texted STOP is refused with `403
  recipient_opted_out`, and someone new to the line counts as a new
  contact on the day you add them, as when you start a group.
- Response: `{"ok": true, "chat": {"chat_id": …, "name": …, "participants": […]}}`,
  the group after the change.

```http
POST /api/imessage/groups/leave
Content-Type: application/json
{"chat_id": "iMessage;+;chat123456789"}
```

Answers `{"ok": true}`. Your number leaves the group: it stops receiving its
messages, and the group drops out of `GET /api/imessage/groups`. The stored
messages stay readable.

## Typing, reactions, replies and polls

- **Typing:** `POST /api/imessage/typing` with `chat_id` instead of `to`
  shows your "…" in the group. When a member types, the `typing` object of
  `typing.started` / `typing.stopped` carries `chat_id`, and its `handle` is
  the member typing.
- **Reactions:** `POST /api/imessage/reactions` with the `message_id` of a
  group message sends the tapback into that group; the stored reaction
  carries `chat_id`. Members' tapbacks arrive with `chat_id` and their own
  `handle`.
- **Replies:** `reply_to` on a group send must name a message in that same
  group (`422 invalid_reply_to` otherwise).
- **Polls:** `poll` with `chat_id` sends a poll to the group; every member
  can vote, and `voters` lists each member's handle.

## Webhooks and the live stream

Group messages arrive as the same events as 1:1 ones (`message.received`,
`message.sent`, `message.failed`, `reaction.*`, `poll.voted`), each
`message` carrying `chat_id`. Two things are new.

**Group events.** A rename, a member added or removed, a member leaving and
a new group photo are stored as a message of `kind: "group_event"` and
delivered as their own event, `group.event` (never `message.received`, so an
auto-responder that acts only on `message.received` never answers one). The
body has `message` like every message event. `handle` is who did it, `text`
says what happened, and `group_event` has the details:

```json
{"id": 72, "kind": "group_event", "direction": "in", "handle": "+18055559876",
 "text": "Renamed the group “Launch crew 2026”", "status": "received", "guid": "9D3F…",
 "ts_ms": 1757400300000, "chat_id": "iMessage;+;chat123456789",
 "group_event": {"type": "renamed", "name": "Launch crew 2026"}}
```

`group_event.type` is `renamed` (with `name`), `member_added` or
`member_removed` (with `handle`, the member added or removed; without
`handle`, **your number** was removed: the group is then left, as with
`/groups/leave`), `member_left`, or `photo_changed`.

```
X-ZeroSettle-Event: group.event
```

**`group.updated`.** Whenever a group's name or members change (you start or
update it, or a group event changes it), the [live stream](/imessage/live-stream-sse)
gets a `group.updated` event with the group as it is now. It is **not sent to
your webhook**: read the group with `GET /api/imessage/groups`, or follow
`group.event` messages.

```
event: group
data: {"type":"group","event":"group.updated","chat":{"chat_id":"iMessage;+;chat123456789","name":"Launch crew 2026",…}}
```

It carries the whole group, so keep the one with the newest `updated_at_ms`.

## MCP

When at least one of your numbers has group chats on, the
[MCP](/imessage/mcp) connector adds them too (an account without them sees the same
tools as before):

| Tool | Arguments | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `send_imessage` | `chat_id?` (instead of `to`) | Sends into a group. |
| `list_messages` | `chat_id?` | One group's messages, each labeled with its sender. |
| `show_typing` | `chat_id?` (instead of `to`) | "…" in a group. |
| `create_group_chat` | `to`, `text`, `name?`, `line?` | Same as `POST /api/imessage/groups`. |
| `list_group_chats` | — | Same as `GET /api/imessage/groups`. |
| `update_group_chat` | `chat_id`, `name?`, `add?`, `remove?`, `leave?` | Same as `/groups/update`, or `/groups/leave` with `leave: true`. |

`list_conversations` shows each group by its name (or its members).

## Errors

| HTTP | `code` | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 403 | `group_chats_not_enabled` | A group call on a number without group chats. Nothing was sent. | Group chats are on for every number; contact ZeroSettle support, which may have turned them off for this one. |
| 404 | `unknown_chat` | No line on your account has that `chat_id`. Nothing was sent. | Use a `chat_id` from `GET /api/imessage/groups` or a message. |
| 409 | `client_update_required` | This line requires an update to support group chats. Nothing was sent. | Try again after the update. |
| 403 | `recipient_opted_out` | A member (or a member you are adding) has an active opt-out. `error.handle` says who. | Remove them from the group, or leave it. |
| 429 | `new_contact_limit` | Starting a group or adding members would exceed the line's new-contact allowance. | On Pro, ask the new contacts to text you first or upgrade to Growth. On Growth, wait `retry_after_s` or add fewer new members. |
| 422 | `not_on_imessage` | A new member isn't on iMessage. | Leave them out. |
| 422 | `invalid_request` | Both or neither of `to` and `chat_id`, fewer than 2 or more than 31 members, a name over 60 characters… `error.fields` lists them. | Fix the input. |

## Limits and compliance

- **Each new person is a new contact.** Starting a group, or adding someone
  to one, counts every member the line has never exchanged a message with as
  a new contact (see [Limits](/imessage/errors-limits-and-compliance#limits)). After
  that, members are known contacts: a 1:1 text to one of them is not new.
- Group messages containing **STOP** are delivered as ordinary messages. A
  member can opt out by texting STOP directly to your number. Sends into any
  group they are in are refused while their opt-out is active.
- **Consent covers everyone.** A group message reaches every member, and
  each of them must have agreed to hear from you
  ([Compliance](/imessage/errors-limits-and-compliance#compliance-you-need-consent)).

## Not covered

- **Broadcast is not a group.** `POST /api/imessage/broadcast` still sends
  each recipient their own 1:1 message; use a group when the members should
  see each other.
- **Only messages sent through ZeroSettle are recorded from your side**: the
  API, MCP and the console.
- **The hosted [AI agent](/imessage/ai-agent) answers in groups** on a number it
  manages: into the group, only when a message is for it, and never to
  another ZeroSettle number; triggers never fire into a group.
- **CRMs:** group messages are not logged in Close or HubSpot.
- **Chat backgrounds** set in a group are not reported.
