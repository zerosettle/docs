<Warning>
  This endpoint is not released yet.
</Warning>

Call this from your server before each AI request. It spends units for one action by one person and answers whether to go ahead. Authenticate with a **secret key** (`zs_sk_test_...` or `zs_sk_live_...`) in the `X-ZeroSettle-Key` header. The key picks the environment; sandbox and live balances never mix. Never put a secret key in an app.

The Node library wraps this call with a 1 second timeout, one retry and an offline answer. See [Usage limits and credits](/iap/usage-limits).

## How units are worked out

- **Fixed action:** its listed cost.
- **Unlisted action:** 1.
- **By-usage action with `amount`:** `amount`. `amount` is ignored for fixed and unlisted actions.
- **By-usage action with `usage` and `model`, or `cost`, when the currency has a cost per unit:** `ceil(cost_usd / cost_per_unit)`, at least 1.
- **By-usage action with none of those:** a hold of `estimate`, else the action's average units over the last 7 days, else 1. Settle a hold with [Finish a Hold](/api-reference/endpoint/finish-usage).

## Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `allowed` | The units are spent (or held). Go ahead. |
| `limit_reached` | Not enough is left. Nothing was spent. |
| `not_in_plan` | The person's plan and add-ons give none of this currency, and they hold no purchased units. Nothing was spent. |

A denied request is still a `200` and still returns a `usage_id`. It is recorded for the live feed and analytics. When the status is not `allowed`, `upgrade_available` carries the upgrade offer or `packs` carries the credit packs, depending on the currency's runs-out setting.

## Errors

Errors look like `{"error": "<code>", "message": "<sentence>"}`.

| Status | Codes |
|---|---|
| `400` | `user_id_required`, `bad_user_id`, `action_required`, `bad_action`, `currency_required`, `unknown_currency`, `bad_amount`, `bad_usage`, `bad_cost`, `bad_estimate`, `no_currencies` |
| `409` | `idempotency_key_reused` |
| `429` | `rate_limited`, with a `Retry-After` header in seconds |

A `401` means the key failed. Treat any `401` as an auth failure.
