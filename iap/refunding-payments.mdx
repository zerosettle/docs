You can refund a one-time or non-subscription purchase made by a customer, or the most recent payment of a customer's subscription.

Refunding returns the funds to the customer's payment method (it can take a few days for the payment to be returned, depending on the payment method and the customer's bank). Access depends on what you refund. A full refund of the current billing period, or of a one-time purchase, ends the customer's access. A partial refund is a credit only and leaves access unchanged. A refund of an earlier period, while a later period is paid and active, leaves the subscription active. See [Refunds](/iap/event-webhooks#refunds) for the events your server receives.

<Note>
**No Refund for Subscriptions with Billing Issues**

For subscriptions where the most recent invoice failed to be collected, you can't refund the payment (since no payment was made). You can still cancel the subscription.
</Note>

## Refunding in the Dashboard

To refund a payment:

1. Navigate to the customer profile in your ZeroSettle dashboard
2. Locate the subscription in the "Entitlements" card
3. Click on the "..." menu next to the subscription
4. Select "Refund"

The refund is processed immediately. For a full refund of the current period, the customer's entitlement is updated: the `isActive` property on the affected `Entitlement` is set to `false`. In every case the corresponding transaction status changes to refunded.
