---
title: "Live stream (SSE)"
---

A server-sent-events feed of everything happening on the line, for a browser
tab or dashboard that wants to watch in real time without hosting a webhook;
the console at `/imessage` is built on it. EventSource cannot send headers,
so mint a short-lived URL token first:

```http
GET /api/imessage/stream-token
```

```bash
curl -s -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" $BASE/api/imessage/stream-token
# {"stream_token":"1757400300.…","expires_at":1757400300,"stream_url":"/api/imessage/stream?device=…&token=…"}
curl -N "$BASE$(…stream_url…)"
```

`stream_url` is relative to the base URL and carries its own auth; `GET
/api/imessage/stream` with a bad or expired token is `401`. The stream opens
with a `: connected` comment and sends a `: ping` comment after every 15 s of
silence.

Events:

```
event: message
data: {"type":"message","event":"message.received","message":{"id":44,"direction":"in",…}}

event: delivery
data: {"type":"delivery","delivery":{"message_id":44,"event":"message.received","url":"https://…","ok":true,"status_code":200,"attempts":1,"error":null,"at":"…"}}

event: typing
data: {"type":"typing","event":"typing.started","typing":{"handle":"+18055559876","state":"start","direction":"in","ts_ms":1757400030000}}

event: conversation
data: {"type":"conversation","event":"conversation.deleted","handle":"+18055559876","deleted":7}

event: optout
data: {"handle":"+18055559876","opted_out_at":"2026-09-28T17:04:11Z"}

event: ai_credits
data: {"type":"ai_credits","ai_credits":{"left":0,"included_left":0,"purchased_left":0,"allowance":1000,"used":1000,"resets_at":null,"status":"exhausted","buy_url":"https://portal.zerosettle.io/imessage#credits"}}
```

`message` carries the same `event` + `message` pair the webhook gets
(`message.*` and `reaction.*`) — the console and your webhook are two
subscribers to one stream. `delivery` reports each webhook attempt as it
happens (retries included). `typing` is always on here (no opt-in needed) and
covers both directions: `direction: "in"` is them typing (the
`typing.started` / `typing.stopped` events), `direction: "out"` is your own
`POST /api/imessage/typing` call, with `expires_at_ms`, so a second console
sees the bubble you raised. `group` carries `group.updated` with the
[group chat](/imessage/group-chats) as it is now (stream only; never sent to a webhook). `conversation` is housekeeping: `conversation.deleted`
after a `DELETE /api/imessage/conversations/{handle}`, and `line.released` if
the operator unassigns the line. `optout` fires when a contact texts STOP, and
again with `"opted_out_at": null` when they text START (see
[STOP is enforced](/imessage/errors-limits-and-compliance#stop-is-enforced)).
`ai_credits` (an account with videos enabled, a line group managed by the
hosted AI agent, or past credit spending or purchases) carries its
[credits](/imessage/ai-agent#credits) when they run low, run out, or more
are bought. The token lives 5 minutes; reconnect with a fresh one.
